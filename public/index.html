    How PingKereta works

    01 · RegisterOwners create a unique QR sticker and verify their notification number once service launches.
    02 · ScanSomeone scans the sticker with a phone camera. No scanner account is needed.
    03 · NotifyThe scanner selects a predefined alert; the owner receives it when notifications are enabled.

    For parking operators

    PingKereta is exploring controlled pilots with malls, parking operators and mobility partners. Operational analytics, verified onboarding and delivery reporting are planned; they are not yet available.
