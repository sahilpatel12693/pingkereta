Three steps. Less parking stress.

A QR sticker creates a private connection between someone who needs help and the vehicle owner.

01 / DAFTAR

Register your vehicle

Approved pilot participants activate their sticker and verify a notification number.

02 / IMBAS

Scan the QR sticker

Anyone with a phone camera can open the alert page. A scanner account is not needed.

03 / MAKLUMKAN

Notify the owner

Choose a reason. When the live service is approved, PingKereta submits a private alert.
